03 / Operating Principles

How I Think, Learn, and Operate

Four mental models and operating guidelines that guide my daily work across investing, accounting, and system building.

01

Understand the WHY

Never accept conclusions at face value. Deconstruct concepts to their fundamental truths, whether it's an accounting ledger rule or a stock valuation spike.

02

Pragmatic Execution

Theory is necessary, but execution builds real competence. Real-world business tests, customer outreach, and cash flow tracking always beat endless academic speculation.

03

Margin of Safety

Protect the downside first. In investing, look for conservative valuations and solid order books; in business, build with lean overhead before scaling.

04

Continuous Compounding

Knowledge, networks, and capital compound at exponential rates. Consistency in reading filings, passing certifications, and building systems creates long-term independence.
